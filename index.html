      Controls

      • Attack + a direction gives a different move. Same for Special and Smash.
      • In the air, Shield is an air dodge. Hold a direction to dodge that way.
      • While shielding: a direction rolls, down sidesteps, Attack grabs, Jump jumps out.
      • Holding someone: press a direction to throw, Attack to hit them.
      • Falling off-stage? Up + Special is your recovery. Grab the ledge, then press a direction to climb.
      • Tap Down after the top of a jump to fast fall. Tap Down on a thin platform to drop through.
      • Getting launched? Press Shield just before hitting the floor to tech.
